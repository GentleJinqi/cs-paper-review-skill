## Candidate findings

For each candidate:

- provisional stable identity inputs;
- primary and related criteria;
- precise claim;
- artifact ID, source anchor, semantic anchor, and observation;
- proposed decision impact and confidence;
- why it matters;
- action type and closure gate;
- verification still needed;
- supporting or opposing evidence;
- dissent, if any.

Candidates are not yet canonical obligations.

## Strengths and clean controls

finding. Do not use generic praise.

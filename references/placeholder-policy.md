# Placeholder And Revision-Markup Policy

Default policy:

- missing quantitative values are not direct defects;
- missing figure assets are not direct defects;
- red text or revision coloring is not a direct defect;
- claims, experiment design, captions, and section logic supported by placeholders remain reviewable;
- final submission readiness remains reviewable;
- numerical results, dataset facts, figure contents, citations, and author decisions must not be invented.

Classify issues around missing author-supplied material as `author-data-gate` or `final-packaging-gate` unless the surrounding claim, structure, or protocol is itself unsupported.

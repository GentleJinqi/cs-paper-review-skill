# Venue conditioning and comparison corpora

Venue conditioning is additive. First complete the portable scientific
assessment. Then apply an exact, source-bound venue profile. Only after those
steps may a paper corpus inform contextual calibration. Corpus evidence cannot
waive a scientific responsibility, prove likely acceptance, or replace an
official scoring field.

Ask for venue, year, and track at intake. `unknown` is valid. A known target
allows the report to use that venue's verified labels, scales, and additional
criteria. A venue-background corpus may describe recurring evidence patterns;
a separately declared topic-near corpus may support comparisons to work with a
similar problem, contribution type, mechanism, claim type, evidence structure,
and modality/application.

Do not use a fixed paper count as a completion gate. Record the search,
screening, deduplication, status authority, and the marginal coverage added by
the latest independently specified batch. Continue while eligible papers or
new topic cells are still being added. If evidence cannot establish
saturation, say so.

Grades `A` and `B` may support material contextual observations when their
status and metadata bindings validate. Grades `C` and `D` remain discovery or
background only. Store metadata and digests, not copied manuscript, review, or
decision text. The machine contract is
`schemas/venue-corpus-manifest.schema.json`; operational guidance and
synthetic examples are in `venue-intelligence/README.md`.

ranges, a twelve-thread configuration example, TMLR as the fallback venue,
and host-specific cleanup language. Those choices are preserved only as
history. They are not evidence of review quality and are not active defaults.

build, loaded configuration, or trustworthy runtime telemetry. Consequently,

claim is inferred from its agent IDs or completion records.
